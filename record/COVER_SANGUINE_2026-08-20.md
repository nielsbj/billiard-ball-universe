# The cover is pulled in sanguine (2026-08-20)

*The thumbnail test of `LAUNCH_PLAN` Phase 0 item 4, run, failed, diagnosed and fixed in one
sitting. Author's ruling: **"sanguine it is."** Cover only — the interior is untouched.*

## The test, as run

Not a description of a shelf but the shelf itself: the front panel cut from the built wrap,
normalised to 135 px tall (at which a 6×9 book is exactly 90 px wide — the test size hit on the
nose), placed sixth in a row with the **ten current Amazon Best Sellers in Physics**, read off the
live page the same day: *Life Time · Thinking in Systems · Six Easy Pieces · Beyond Darwin · Take Me
To Your Leader · Astrophysics for People in a Hurry · Cosmos · My Big TOE · Sapiens · Universe in
Creation.* Sheets in `nbj/`: `real_shelf.png`, `colour_shelf.png`, `colour_big.png`.

**Result against the plan's own rule** (*"Title reads and the ball draws the eye → publish as-is"*):
the title **passed** — legible, better than several neighbours, and the pale ground made it the only
breath of air in a row of dark covers. The ball **failed**. Author's verdict: *"much is good, but
gray is a bit gray, even if it is clear."*

## The diagnosis, which cost one wrong answer first

My first read was that the ground was too close to paper-white, and four deeper grounds were built
and shelved. That was **wrong, and the builds showed it**: a deeper ground produced a better-dressed
blank. Recorded because the record should show the wrong turn.

Colour direction was then taken from a Fable agent, which found the fault properly:

> at 90 px the hatching optically averages to a mid-grey gradient with a white glare, and it stops
> reading as an engraving at all — it reads as a photographic chrome ball-bearing, a stock 3D
> render. And neutral grey sitting on warm cream is a temperature clash: the art looks like a
> photocopy pasted onto nice paper.

The mechanism is measurable. The master is monochrome and strongly **bimodal**: 40.2 % of the
drawing below 0.25 luminance, 46.3 % above 0.78, **13.6 % in between**. At thumbnail size detail
dissolves and the eye lands on the optical average — the ramp's midpoint — which is exactly where
this drawing has nothing. So the fix is not value, it is the **midtone**.

## What was rejected, and on what evidence

All three alternatives were **built as real covers**, not described:

| direction | outcome |
|---|---|
| deeper ground alone (4 shades tried) | better-dressed blank; ball still a grey blob |
| **louder oxblood** — ground + oxblood subtitle + 2 pt rule, art untouched | genuine improvement, but the ball stays grey. Kept on file as the low-risk option |
| **intaglio blue** — one blue-black plate ink throughout | **predicted in advance** to stay chromatically adjacent to grey and half-solve it; at 90 px proved indistinguishable from as-built. The prediction landing is part of why the recommendation was trusted |
| **Concept B, the dark plate** (`BOOK_COVER.md` had pre-authorised mocking it if A disappointed) | inversion turns the ball's highlight into a shadow — a billiard ball reads as a void. Doing it properly means re-lighting the engraving. Rejected; noted in `BOOK_COVER.md` |

## What was applied

- **Ground** `#F4EFE4` → `#EDE4CE`.
- **Plate** printed in sanguine: tritone ramp `#331B17` → `#7E4437` → `#FAF4E7`.
- **Oxblood rule** 1.1 pt → 1.8 pt. At 90 px a 1.1 pt rule is invisible, so the cover's only note of
  colour was doing no work in the only view most buyers ever get.
- **Title left pure `#1A1A1A`.** Its contrast at thumbnail size is the cover's largest single asset
  and must not be spent on colour.

**The master artwork was not modified.** `cover_recursive_ball.png` is read only. The derivative
`cover_recursive_ball_sanguine.png` is generated by the new committed `cover/gen_sanguine.py` —
delete it and re-run to rebuild it byte-for-byte, exactly the pattern the barcode already uses. The
drawing itself is unchanged: this is the same plate pulled in red ink.

**On identity.** Paper plus one printer's ink is the oldest convention in engraving, and sanguine
plates are period-correct, so the book's monochrome character survives; the interior stays grayscale
by policy. One hue is a treatment, two would be decoration — `BOOK_COVER.md` now says so.

## Open

- **`\PAGECOUNT` still 182** against a 202 pp interior. Unchanged on purpose; the spine is computed
  last, after the two plate rulings. **The cover built here is still not the one to upload.**
- **Proof-day watch-list**, now written into `BOOK_COVER.md`: dark reds shift brown in CMYK and the
  hatched field carries more ink than black-on-near-white did — the midtone is deliberately
  desaturated to survive that, so resist pushing it redder. A flat cream field across the whole wrap
  can band or show handling marks. The old check *"ivory right"* becomes **"parchment right,
  sanguine not maroon."**
- The ramp endpoints are Fable's first recommendation, applied as given. A tuning pass on the three
  endpoints against the built result has **not** been done and is the obvious next refinement if the
  proof or a second look wants one.
