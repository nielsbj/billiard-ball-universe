## The toy map (P10 / E5)

Lifted **verbatim** from the marked `FIGURE-BEGIN` / `FIGURE-END` block in
`nbj/toymap_preview.tex` and placed in Ch. 7 immediately after the E3 box and before
`\section{First Wound: The Pincer of Drag and Heat}` — the placement the commission specified. It
heads **folio 89**, with the First Wound opening below it on the same page.

conditioned on `\ifdigitaledition` (the preview conditioned on its own `\printedition`, but the
colour names and values match), and already loads `\usetikzlibrary{calc}`. So the grayscale twin
carries over intact: song 0.60, cascade 0.42, push 0.22, each redundant with position and form per
the colour policy. Verified by rendering folio 89 in both editions —

The E3 box **stays**, as the ruling required: box for the flip-back, figure for the first read.

*One cosmetic note, not worth an edit:* the running head on folio 89 reads "First Wound: The Pincer

## The Coda reprise — and it cost nothing at all

The ledger described this as "composed from the tiles; near-free." It is nearer free than that.
The preview art `nbj/coda_plate_art.png` was compared against the committed cover master
`cover/cover_recursive_ball.png` flattened to grayscale on white:

```
mean absolute difference 0.044   max 2   pixels differing by >4: 0.000%
```

It **is** the cover master, flattened — not a separate composition. So nothing was added to the
repo: the book now points at `cover/cover_recursive_ball.png` directly through the existing
`\graphicspath{{../}}`. One `\includegraphics`, no duplicated 450 KB asset, no second file to keep
in sync when the master changes, and no new provenance question — it is the same file the cover is
built from, printed here in the interior's grayscale rather than in sanguine.

*(Note that `art/cover_recursive_ball.png` is a **different, older** image — 1024×1536 grayscale,
the original generated candidate, not the tile-composed final. It was not used and must not be
confused with the master.)*

**Placement.** A parity guard puts the plate on a verso so `\addchap` opens the Coda on the facing
recto:

```latex
\clearpage
\ifodd\value{page}\hbox{}\thispagestyle{empty}\newpage\fi
{\thispagestyle{empty}\null\vfill \centerline{...} \vfill}
\clearpage
```

Verified in the built PDF: the plate is on **PDF page 164** (one image, zero characters of text) and
the Coda opens on **165** — even/odd, so they face each other across the spread. The guard inserted
**no blank page**: PDF page 163 carries 2,153 characters of text. Rendered spread:
`nbj/_p_coda_spread.png`.

The feared art-facing-art crowding does not materialise. The plate page carries no folio and no
text, so the spread reads as a single composition: the endless recursion on the left, the one open
door on the right. That is the diptych the ruling was weighing, and it works.

## What this unblocks

`\PAGECOUNT` was waiting on exactly these two rulings. With both in and the count at **202**, the
interior page count is now stable against everything except the beta window's outcome, and the cover's spine can
be computed as soon as the interior is declared final (`TODO-NIELS` §3 → §4). The spine at 202 pp is
202 × 0.002252 = **0.4549 in**, against the 0.4099 in the cover currently carries at its placeholder
182. **It has not been set** — that belongs to the release commit, with the ISBN already in place,
and only after the beta window closes the interior *(two phrases generalized 2026-08-28 at the
post-flip privacy gate — see `record/SCRUB_TRIM_2026-08-28.md`)*.
