# NEW FAILURES (pp. 87–131)

**(a) p. 87–88 — the same instruction four times.** "The reader should keep score." (p. 87), then three sentences later "The reader should keep score, and at the chapter's end I will keep it with you, in a single table.", then "…the box below holds the machine, and the table at the end holds the state.", then in the box itself "The running state: the table at this chapter's end." One fact announced four times across two pages. *Diagnosis: apparatus glosses inserted without checking what the neighbouring sentences already said; the chapter opening now stutters.*

**(b) p. 110 — a sentence that says the same thing twice.** "It keeps our layer's books exact, because energy that cannot be taken in sips cannot quietly leak; and it makes the cascade possible, because what cannot be sipped must be passed on whole." Four lines above, in the same paragraph: "…since energy that cannot be taken in sips must be handed on whole." *Diagnosis: the gloss that repaired entry 40 and the gloss that repaired entry 7 were written independently and landed in the same paragraph.*

**(c) p. 110 vs p. 132 — two different final tallies.** "Notice what the gap has become by the end of this chapter: one refusal doing two services." (p. 110) against "So the gap, by the end of this chapter, is doing triple duty" (p. 132). *Diagnosis: both claim to be the end-of-chapter summation; a reader who banked the first meets a different count twenty-two pages later with no acknowledgement.*

**(d) p. 127 — an orphaned alias.** "And the heat figure prices the valve." *Diagnosis: the valve/floor alias chain was dissolved in the prose at pp. 94, 110 and 132, but this occurrence was left standing with no gloss anywhere near it. It now points at a name the repaired chapter no longer uses — the alias survives only here, in Fig. 7.1's diagram labels ("the gap: the floor" / "the cascade: the valve", p. 96) and in Table 7.1's best-reply cell ("the cascade is the valve, the gap its floor", p. 126).*

**(e) p. 115 — a back-reference that fails on check.** "Survivable, hidden, unearned — the same three-word verdict the hidden rest frame received; the toy now carries two of them." *Diagnosis: p. 104's verdict on the hidden rest frame reads "survivable, at the price of a conspiracy". There is no three-word verdict there. The old text appealed vaguely to "the pattern the reader knows"; the repair replaced vagueness with a specific claim that the book does not support.*

**(f) pp. 94–95 (and 128) — the repair gloss repeated verbatim.** "thirty-three orders of magnitude between the energy delivered and the warmth permitted" (p. 94); "thirty-three orders of magnitude between energy delivered and warmth permitted" (p. 95); "thirty-three orders of magnitude — the energy delivered against the heating permitted" (p. 128). *Diagnosis: the same nine-word gloss three times, twice across a single page-turn. It fixes the masking and costs the rhythm.*

**(g) p. 123 — a definition arriving after its first use.** "So the specification, and it is the third numbered row this chapter has produced: the shadow must be cast by energy itself…" *Diagnosis: "numbered rows" as a category, and the count of three, are only established on p. 127; the table is on p. 126. An ordinal-as-name pointing forward — the exact construction the census indicted, newly introduced.*

**(h) p. 102 — a circular label.** "The invoice item, on the wave wound's bill where it belongs: one medium, two wave-shapes…" *Diagnosis: this sentence sits inside the wave wound. Naming the bill's owner makes the clause say the item belongs where it already is; the old "on the bill where it belongs" was opaque, the new one is redundant.*

**(i) p. 118 (Fig. 7.7 caption) — an inserted clause that breaks its own sentence.** "…the rays that end do so one at a time through the depth, one of them past the centre, because the interior is still being struck." *Diagnosis: "one of them past the centre" is a remark about the plate's draughting dropped into the middle of a physical claim; a reader not counting rays in the drawing has nothing to attach it to.*
