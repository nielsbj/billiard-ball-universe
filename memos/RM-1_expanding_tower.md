## 1. The thought, with its one correction
As stated, the signature holds for matter and needs one correction for light. A photon cannot lose *speed* — in the crowd picture the medium pins c, the wave speed of the crowd. What a photon loses is frequency. For massive particles the stated sign is exactly right: momentum measured against the comoving crowd decays. One law covers both: **p ∝ 1/a** — for slow bodies this reads v̇ = −Hv; for light it reads ν ∝ 1/a at fixed c; for cosmic rays it is the standard adiabatic loss.

## 2. What this re-houses, rather than predicts
That law is not new physics — it is textbook cosmology. A particle coasting relative to the comoving frame genuinely slows, at the Hubble rate, in everyone's cosmology. So the claim here is not a fresh curve; it is **ontological**: recession is real motion through the endless nothing; what dilutes is the crowd at every layer, not a stretching nothing; "expanding space" is bookkeeping for crowd dilution plus genuine velocities. There is a respectable literature holding that the kinematic reading of cosmological redshift is coordinate-for-coordinate legitimate [VERIFY-1: Bunn & Hogg, *Am. J. Phys.* 77, 688 (2009); Francis, Barnes, James & Lewis, *PASA* (2007)]. The memo stands on that debate instead of re-fighting it.

## 3. Two consonances inside the toy
**(a) The passenger gets a face.** The second wound's standing price is a rest frame that local experiment can never see; the fifth wound's channels run "in the frame already bought and hidden." Cosmology hands us exactly one preferred frame at the largest scale: the comoving frame, read off the sky as the frame in which the microwave background is isotropic. Identify them. The conspiracy still does its local work — no glassware sees a wind — but the sky does: our dipole speed of about 370 km/s [VERIFY-2: Planck value ≈ 369.8 km/s] becomes, in this reading, our present speed through the crowd. The metaphysical passenger was never absent; it was outdoors.

**(b) The drag gets a job; the valve gets a second audit row.** The first wound's poison — motion through the crowd costs momentum — is here employed at the gentlest possible rate, H ≈ 2.3×10⁻¹⁸ per second ≈ 7×10⁻¹¹ per year [VERIFY-3: H₀ taken as 70 km/s/Mpc; state the choice], with the energy sold downward through the fourth wound's gap. And it can be audited exactly the way the State of the Wounds audits the pincer. Earth's kinetic energy in the comoving frame is about 4×10³⁵ J; the drain 2HE is then about 1.9×10¹⁸ W. None of that may surface as heat here. Ceiling: the planet's entire internal outflow, 4.7×10¹³ W (daviesdavies2010, already in the book's bibliography). So the drag's **local-deposition efficiency must be below 2.5×10⁻⁵** — and since geology already accounts for its 47 TW to within a couple of terawatts, the honest ceiling sits nearer one part in a million. A second specification row, independent of the first: the pincer row bounded the valve's leak at one part in 10³³ of the intake; this row bounds the Hubble drain's local deposit at parts in 10⁵–10⁶ of itself. Both are demands the basement must meet, not evidence that it exists.

One more consonance comes free. The wounds table prices the first wound at "energy books close only regressively — no first account." The oldest embarrassment of expanding-universe pedagogy — *where does the redshifted energy go?* — receives, in the tower, the same verdict the table already printed: one floor down, forever.

## 4. Three test families, three debts
**(i) Bound systems — the hard one.** Full-strength H-drag on orbital motion decays orbits at da/dt = 2Ha ≈ 21 m per year ≈ 2.1 km per century at 1 AU (derivation in §6). Planetary ephemerides bound anomalous drift at the centimetres-per-year scale [VERIFY-4: Pitjeva & Pitjev; also note Krasinsky & Brumberg's 2004 claim of 15 m/century and its later absorption]. That is an exclusion of roughly **two to three orders of magnitude** — and a correction to this memo's own seed: the chat estimate said "one to two orders"; built out, it is worse, and the correction is recorded here. So bound systems must be exempt, as they are in standard cosmology — but there the exemption is a coordinate fact, and here it is a **mechanism owed**: the crowd must co-move with bound structure. The crux, stated honestly: the Lorentz conspiracy can hide a frame from clocks and rulers, but a momentum drain is dynamics — it cannot be hidden, only absent. The debt is a crowd that clumps with matter. (A crowd that clumps with matter is, incidentally, the shape of the dark-matter door the book left open. Noted, not spent.)

**(ii) Constants — the discipline.** If dilution is real, dimensionful drift is invisible by construction; only dimensionless ratios can betray it. The fine-structure constant is steady to parts per million over gigayears — the book's own quasar anchor — and to roughly 10⁻¹⁷–10⁻¹⁹ per year in atomic clocks [VERIFY-5: Rosenband et al. 2008; the 2021 clock-comparison bounds]. H-linear tracking would drift at 7×10⁻¹¹ per year. The model therefore owes: every measured dimensionless ratio is dilution-blind to at least six or eight orders. In-toy this is natural — α is a ratio of same-crowd quantities — but natural is not derived, and the memo does not pretend otherwise.

**(iii) The graveyard — defense pre-loaded.** "Particles slowly lose energy" pattern-matches tired light, which is dead three times over: supernova light curves stretch by (1+z) [VERIFY-6: Goldhaber et al. 2001], the microwave background stays a blackbody with T ∝ (1+z) [VERIFY-7: Noterdaeme et al. 2011 and/or Fixsen], and surface brightness obeys Tolman. This model survives because it is not tired light: the velocities are real, so the time dilation is real Doppler dilation; the frequency decay is the p ∝ 1/a of a kinematic universe, not en-route dissipation; nothing scatters, so nothing blurs. By construction the model reproduces the standard curves — and the moment it fails to, it dies by the same three shots. That sentence is the memo's insurance policy, printed in advance.

## 5. What this buys, and what it does not
Buys: an ontology — expansion without an expanding nothing; two unifications inside the toy — the hidden frame identified with the comoving frame, the pincer's poison employed as the Hubble drain; one new audit row for the specification; and a research question with edges — the entrainment mechanism of (i). Does not buy: any new observational curve at current precision — this is standard cosmology in different ontological clothes, and that admission belongs in bold, not in a referee report. Does not buy: dark energy — the book's line stands, "Marked holes are more useful than papered ones" — and it will not be sold against the Hubble tension either; that hole stays marked. One loose thread, filed as a question and not a claim: in a diluting tower the specification's speed floor (24×10¹⁷ c) becomes an epoch-dependent number, and nothing yet measures its drift.
