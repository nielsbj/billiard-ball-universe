ANCHOR — 2026-08-24 — the P22 cover (OTS interim)
================================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
An INTERIM protection stamp, in the sense of the OpenTimestamps runbook's Rule 2.
It is NOT the release anchor. The release anchor (OTS-3) remains a separate,
later event, tied to the tag `first-edition-1.0` and fired only after the
physical proof is approved.

WHY IT EXISTS
-------------
Patch P22, hours after P21, changed one word in the back-cover blurb: "As a
child, Niels Bonde Jensen DECIDED that two things were cheating" became
"... FELT that two things were cheating" — the book's own staging ("I felt it
then and I will say it stronger now"), which the blurb had compressed. P22
substitutes a word, so the new sentence lies outside every earlier anchor.
This stamp covers the new cover source and the new wrap PDF, and nothing
else. The manuscript, ref.bib and both interior PDFs are unchanged since the
2026-08-21 anchor and are not re-stamped.

A WARNING ABOUT THE PDF
-----------------------
pdflatex embeds a build timestamp, so rebuilding from the same source does NOT
reproduce these bytes. The PDF proof is only worth something while that exact
file survives. If it is lost, nothing important is lost — the source is
anchored, committed, and byte-exact in git forever.
