ANCHOR — 2026-08-24 — the P24 interior (OTS interim)
================================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
An INTERIM protection stamp, in the sense of the OpenTimestamps runbook's Rule 2.
It is NOT the release anchor. The release anchor (OTS-3) remains a separate,
later event, tied to the tag `first-edition-1.0` and fired only after the
physical proof is approved.

WHY IT EXISTS
-------------
On KDP upload day the previewer rejected the print interior: the folio sat
13 pt from the bottom trim, inside KDP's 18 pt minimum, on 191 of 206 pages.
Patch P24 fixed it with one geometry line (footskip=36pt); not one word of
the book changed, and the pagination was proven identical page by page. Both
interiors are new bytes, and the day's rule is kept simple: every restaged
artifact carries a fresh anchor. This stamp covers the new manuscript source
and both new interior PDFs, and nothing else. ref.bib and the P23 cover are
unchanged and not re-stamped.

A WARNING ABOUT THE PDFs
------------------------
pdflatex embeds a build timestamp, so rebuilding from the same source does NOT
reproduce these bytes. The PDF proofs are only worth something while those
exact files survive. If they are lost, nothing important is lost — the source
is anchored, committed, and byte-exact in git forever.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
-------------------
- Not the published edition. No tag exists; the repository is private; no
  proof copy has been printed or approved.
- Not a claim that this interior is unchanged from here. Proof findings are
  expected and may change it; they change the price of a correction, not its
  possibility.
- Not a substitute for OTS-3.

This file is frozen. It is itself stamped, so editing it after stamping would
break its own proof.
