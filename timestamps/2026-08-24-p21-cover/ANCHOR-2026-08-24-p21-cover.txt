ANCHOR — 2026-08-24 — the P21 cover (OTS interim)
================================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
An INTERIM protection stamp, in the sense of the OpenTimestamps runbook's Rule 2.
It is NOT the release anchor. The release anchor (OTS-3) remains a separate,
later event, tied to the tag `first-edition-1.0` and fired only after the
physical proof is approved.

WHY IT EXISTS
-------------
Patch P21 changed one sentence on the back cover: "Where the toy universe
breaks, the breaks are shown" became "Where the world wounds the toy, the
wounds are shown" — the verdict the wounds table actually supports. Unlike P20,
which only deleted, P21 ADDS words, and the 2026-08-21 anchor cannot say
"not later than" about words it never contained. This stamp covers the new
cover source and the new wrap PDF, and nothing else. The manuscript, ref.bib
and both interior PDFs are unchanged since the 2026-08-21 anchor and are not
re-stamped.

A WARNING ABOUT THE PDF
-----------------------
pdflatex embeds a build timestamp, so rebuilding from the same source does NOT
reproduce these bytes. The PDF proof is only worth something while that exact
file survives. If it is lost, nothing important is lost — the source is
anchored, committed, and byte-exact in git forever.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
-------------------

- Not a claim that this cover is unchanged from here. Proof findings are

  possibility.
- Not a substitute for OTS-3.
